The junior spots, predicts, compares

Spot

“Anna changed three things in your email. What do you see?”

Jonas finds two of them.

Predict

“Why do you think she removed the apology?”

He answers before he can see hers.

Compare

“You saw both reasons. One change you missed: the CFO moved to To.”

The miss is the real lesson.

Private to the junior. Encouraging, never graded.

Tacit · How it works

10

Step 3, the junior side. The order matters: spot first, then predict, then compare. Predicting before seeing the answer is what makes it stick, and it builds the habit of noticing expert moves. Jonas spotted the ask and the apology but missed the Cc change. That miss, with Anna’s reason (“she owns the budget, so she’s the one we’re asking”), is the most valuable thing he learns that day. Only Jonas sees this comparison.
