The problem

Juniors see the result, not the reasoning

Jonas’s draft to the client

“Apologies for the delay on our side… It would be great if you could possibly share the Q3 volume data per depot when you get a chance.”

What Anna actually sent

“To keep the SteerCo on 14 Nov on track, we need the Q3 volume data per depot by Thursday 7 Nov.”

Three judgment calls happened in that rewrite. Jonas sees none of them.

Tacit · How it works

2

A typical moment. Jonas, an analyst, drafts a data request to the client. Anna, the engagement manager, rewrites it: the ask goes in the first line with a deadline, the apology disappears, and the CFO moves from Cc to To. Jonas only sees the sent version. The reasoning, which is the valuable part, is never said out loud.
