“It depends” becomes an explicit rule

In the prep call, Anna said

“Whether we show the downside… it depends who’s in the room.”

Tacit asked what decides it. Anna answered with a 14-second voice note.

Heuristic · conditional

If the CFO attends, show the downside scenario upfront. If only operations attend, keep it in the appendix.

Anna Lindqvist · SteerCo #3 – Halvorsen · approved 5 Nov

Three formats: decisions, heuristics (when, do, because, except) and short cases. Nothing is shared until the senior approves it.

Tacit · How it works

11

Steps 4 and 5. Capture: answers come in by one tap, a reason tag or a short voice note, which Tacit transcribes. Distil: Tacit turns them into structured knowledge. Here, a vague “it depends” becomes a conditional rule with the conditions spelled out. Every item carries its provenance: who said it, on which task, when. And it stays a draft until the senior approves it.
