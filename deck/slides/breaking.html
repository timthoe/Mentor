The problem

Apprenticeship is quietly breaking down

Hybrid work

No team room, no cab ride, no partner on the phone at the next desk to overhear.

No time to explain

Managers run several workstreams. Explanations get postponed, then forgotten.

Unknown unknowns

Juniors often don’t notice that a decision was made, so they don’t ask.

KM comes too late

Writing it up afterwards rarely happens, and it loses the context when it does.

Tacit · How it works

3

Consulting runs on a leverage model: juniors learn judgment by working next to managers and partners. Four forces are weakening that. Hybrid work removes overhearing. Seniors have no time. Juniors don’t know what they don’t know. And knowledge management asks people to document after the fact, which they don’t, and which loses the context anyway. Every departure takes judgment with it.
