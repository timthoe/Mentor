The biggest risk is that Tacit feels like surveillance. These principles are product rules, not just policy. “Never evaluation” also matters legally: under the EU AI Act, AI used to evaluate employees is high-risk, so Tacit is built and positioned not to do that. Add GDPR (DPIA, data minimisation, EU hosting), consulting the works council early, and client confidentiality through redaction and access walls.
