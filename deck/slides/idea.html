The core idea

Two questions at the right moment

To the senior

“You led with the ask and removed the apology. Why?”

Answered in seconds: confirm Tacit’s guess, pick a reason, or record a voice note.

To the junior

“Anna changed three things in your email. Can you spot them, and guess why?”

Predict first, then see the senior’s reasoning side by side.

The answers become approved, reusable knowledge for the next team.

Tacit · How it works

5

Tacit doesn’t teach content itself. It creates the conditions for apprenticeship. The senior is asked to make thinking visible, at near-zero cost. The junior is asked to notice and predict before seeing the answer, which is what makes it stick. This builds on cognitive apprenticeship (modelling, articulation, reflection) and on Nonaka’s point that turning tacit into explicit knowledge is the hard step. Tacit does it during the work, not after.
