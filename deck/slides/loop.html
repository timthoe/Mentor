How it works

Six steps, one loop

1 Observe

Follows mail, Teams chat, meeting transcripts and file versions for one opted-in task.

2 Detect

Spots teachable moments: decisions, corrections, cuts and “it depends”.

3 Prompt

Asks the senior why, and asks the junior to notice and predict.

4 Capture

Records answers in seconds: one tap, a reason tag or a short voice note.

5 Distil

Turns answers into heuristics, decisions and cases. The senior approves each one.

6 Resurface

Shows the right knowledge when someone faces a similar task.

Every approved item makes the next answer, and the next prompt, smarter.
